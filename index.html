Research

Book project

My current book project, Filling the Void: The Bank of Italy and the Politics of Central Banking in an Age of Crisis, 1963–1981, examines the political role of the Bank of Italy during the turbulent decades that reshaped the Italian economy and state. It explores how the central bank came to act beyond a narrow monetary function, intervening in moments of institutional weakness and helping to redefine the boundaries of central banking itself.

Publications

I publish on the history and political economy of central banking, credit policy, and economic governance, and I also write shorter pieces for a wider policy audience. A selection of publications is listed below.

Academic publications

  • How Credit Policy Ends: Central Bankers and the Rise and Fall of Credit Controls in Italy, 1973–1983
    A study of how Italian central bankers managed the transition away from postwar credit controls and what this tells us about the changing role of central banks in credit policy.
  • A European Credit Council? Lessons from the History of Italian Central Banking after World War II
    This article revisits postwar Italian experience with credit policy to discuss proposals for democratising the European Central Bank's role in credit allocation.
